### Boundaries of success
**Passed**: PSD byte parser, actual raster layers, alpha information, tag organization, mesh texture uploads, head motion, CPU/SwiftShader frame capture and real 24fps video assembly.
**Not established**: exact See-through output compatibility/version; correct aesthetic draw order/occlusion; reconstructed hidden anatomy; artistic 48fps uniqueness (only 21 unique frames); visual fidelity and seams; blink/mouth expressions; user anime character approval.
No downloaded model checkpoints or paid inference.

### Unexpected additional Japanese open-source lead
[852wa/Anime2.5DRig](https://github.com/852wa/Anime2.5DRig/) is MIT-licensed and purpose-built for PSD auto-rigging, iris masks, eye/mouth swaps, hair-strand spring physics, and browser animation. Evaluate **as an independent alternative** and avoid making two parallel large engines.
